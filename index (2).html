Section 4.1 — Core Design Principles

1. Buttons First, Free Text Always

Structured WhatsApp interactive messages reduce parsing errors and token costs. Voice notes and custom text are always processed without rejection.

2. Three Taps to Reorder

A returning customer reaches payment in 3 interactions or fewer. Single most critical performance metric.

3. Echo Before Charge

Order, items, delivery address, and total price restated in one message before issuing payment details.

4. Clear Next Action Always

No dead ends. Every message explicitly signals whose turn it is and what action is required.
